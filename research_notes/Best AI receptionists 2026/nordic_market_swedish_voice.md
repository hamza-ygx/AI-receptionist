# Nordic AI Receptionist Market and Swedish-Language Voice AI (2025–2026)

How the research was done: research date 2026-10-16. Direct page fetches (WebFetch/curl) were blocked by the session's egress policy for every target host tried (telavox.se, svarstjanster.se, svaria.io, kb-labb.github.io). All findings below therefore come from search-engine result extracts of the cited pages, not from full-page reads. Treat exact numbers as "as reported in search extracts" and re-check them on the live pages before relying on them. The shared web-search budget ran out before the planned follow-ups (PTS caller-ID rules, the Article 50 wording, the ElevenLabs language list, the GPT-SW3/Viking status, Weblink/Flexi SIP docs). Those are listed under Gaps.

## 1. Nordic vendors and products (AI receptionist / AI telefonist / AI-växel)

### Takeaway
By 2026 the Swedish SMB market has many players. The operators and PBX vendors (Telavox, Tele2, Telink) now sell AI receptionists as add-ons to their cloud PBX. Many small Swedish startups (Svaria, Menodi, Vibeforge, TechTell, Techgruppen, Skaala, SARA, M8Com, Tell) sell flat-fee products at roughly 300–4,500 SEK/month. Almost all quality, GDPR and hosting claims are vendor marketing. I found no independent Swedish-quality benchmark or third-party review. Svaria is the only vendor found with content aimed at redovisningsbyråer, and it is a marketing blog.

### Inferences
- The flat-fee SMB products (about 800–2,000 SEK/month) mostly look like white-label wrappers on global voice-agent stacks (Vapi/Retell/ElevenLabs-style). I infer this from pricing and the 29–90 language claims, which match those platforms' language lists, but I did not verify it. A self-hosted stack competes on control, data residency and deep integrations (Fortnox/Visma/Capego status lookups), not on price.
- The accounting-firm use case vendors pitch (status lookups by orgnr, consultant booking, deadline reminders) matches what a Halmstad audit firm would need. None of them shows evidence of handling the tystnadsplikt issue (see section 5).
- Operators bundling AI receptionists (Tele2/Responda, Telavox, Telink) will be the default comparison for a firm already on those PBXs, at about 500–2,000 SEK/month.

## 2. Integrating Swedish PBX (växel) providers with an external AI agent (SIP forwarding, caller ID)

### Takeaway
All mainstream Swedish PBXs support forwarding a number or queue to an external number, so the simplest integration is forwarding to the AI's Twilio/46elks/Telnyx DID. I found no public documentation on native SIP-URI forwarding to a third-party AI or on caller-ID passthrough for Telavox, Telia Touchpoint, Tele2 Växel, Telenor, Weblink or Flexi. That has to be confirmed with each provider.

### Cited Findings
- Telavox supports direct, delayed (no answer) and busy forwarding to a chosen number, configured per profile in the admin/user interface. Forwarding settings apply to landline numbers, and reserved numbers can also be forwarded. — [Telavox support: Vidarekoppla samtal](https://support.telavox.com/sv_SE/kontakter1/vidarekoppla-samtal); [Telavox support: Plustjänster](https://support.telavox.com/sv_SE/mobilanvandare/plustjanster)
- Telavox's AI receptionist can be added directly in the PBX (native, not SIP). Calls it cannot handle are transferred to a human or the business (per a third-party blog). — [Svaria blog](https://www.svaria.io/blogg/ai-receptionist-eller-mansklig-vaxel-nar-passar-vad); [Telavox UK PBX](https://telavox.co.uk/pbx/)
- Telia Touchpoint Plus can be integrated over SIP trunk groups with Telia's own ACE IVR/dialler (an enterprise contact-centre path). — [Telia ACE 21 release notes](https://docs.ace.teliacompany.com/downloads/21/ACE21ReleaseNotes.pdf)
- Third-party AI receptionists (TechTell, Telink) say they "connect to the number you already have". Skaala requires disabling operator voicemail, which implies a conditional call-forward to the AI number. — [TechTell](https://techtell.se/ai-receptionist); [Telink](https://telink.se/en/); [Skaala Tele2 guide](https://www.skaala.ai/sv/guides/rostbrevlada/tele2)
- Twilio Sweden voice guidelines: outbound caller ID is "local (not guaranteed)" for domestic calls. — [Twilio Sweden voice guidelines](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/guidelines/se/voice)

### Inferences
- With PSTN forwarding (PBX to Twilio DID), the original caller's number is usually presented when the PBX supports "nummerpresentation av ursprunglig uppringare" on forwarded calls. Some PBXs present the forwarding number instead. I could not confirm which behaviour each provider uses. Test it and inspect the SIP `From` and `Diversion`/`History-Info` headers in LiveKit/Twilio.
- For a firm on Telavox or Tele2, the main question is: forward to the self-hosted AI number, or buy the native add-on (about 1,600 SEK/month for Tele2)?
- When transferring back from the AI to a human, a SIP REFER or PSTN transfer to the PBX's direct numbers will also face caller-ID presentation rules. Twilio says Swedish outbound caller ID is not guaranteed.

### Gaps
- No documentation retrieved for Weblink, Flexi (Flexi/Weblink), Telenor Växel or Telia Smart Connect on SIP-URI forwarding or caller-ID passthrough on forwarded calls.
- PTS rules on caller-ID spoofing and blocking (relevant to presenting the original caller's number on outbound legs) were not retrieved; the search budget ran out.

## 3. Swedish speech-to-text (STT): quality and benchmarks

### Takeaway
No independent head-to-head Swedish WER benchmark covering Azure, Google, Deepgram, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, AssemblyAI and Speechmatics exists in the sources, and none on 8 kHz telephony audio. The only rigorous open Swedish numbers are KBLab's. KB-Whisper-large gets 5.4 / 4.1 / 5.2 WER on FLEURS / Common Voice / NST, about 47% lower than Whisper-large-v3. It is a batch Whisper model, not a native streaming one. Among commercial streaming engines with Swedish: Azure sv-SE got a model update in August 2026. Deepgram has a Nova-3 Swedish monolingual model. AssemblyAI reports Swedish FLEURS WER of 6.6% for Universal-3.6 Pro (vendor figure). ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Realtime and Speechmatics also support Swedish.

### Cited Findings
- **KB-Whisper (KBLab, National Library of Sweden, released 7 March 2025; Interspeech 2025 paper "Swedish Whispers").** Its best model cuts WER by about 47% on average versus OpenAI whisper-large-v3 across FLEURS, Common Voice and NST. WER table (KBLab vs OpenAI, FLEURS / CV / NST):
  - tiny 13.2/12.9/11.2 vs 59.2/67.8/85.2
  - base 9.1/8.7/7.8 vs 39.6/52.1/53.4
  - small 7.3/6.4/6.6 vs 20.6/26.4/26.4
  - medium 6.6/5.4/5.8 vs 12.1/15.8/17.1
  - large-v3 5.4/4.1/5.2 vs 7.8/9.5/11.3
  — [arXiv 2505.17538](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.17538v1); [Interspeech 2025 PDF](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/vesterbacka25_interspeech.pdf); [HF: KBLab/kb-whisper-large](https://huggingface.co/KBLab/kb-whisper-large); [KBLab blog](https://kb-labb.github.io/posts/2025-03-07-welcome-KB-Whisper/)
- KB-Whisper was trained on about 50,000 hours of Swedish speech (per a third-party model listing). — [aibase model page](https://model.aibase.com/models/details/1915693359521488897)
- **Azure Speech (sv-SE):** the August 2026 release notes list a speech-to-text model update covering Swedish (one of ten languages, with English kept for bilingual use). It is described as improving transcription quality and entity recognition, with more accurate multilingual results. Swedish was also added to "monolingual post-stream refinement" (now 29 locales). Swedish is available for fast transcription. A docs.azure.cn mirror does not show this item. — [Azure Speech release notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/speech-service/releasenotes); [Azure language support](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/speech-service/language-support); [docs.azure.cn release notes](https://docs.azure.cn/en-us/ai-services/speech-service/releasenotes)
- **Deepgram Nova-3:** monolingual Swedish (`sv`, `sv-SE`) model for batch and streaming, announced with German, Dutch and Danish (about 13 months before the search, so around autumn 2025). Language detection is not supported in streaming, so set `language=sv` explicitly or use the multilingual model. Swedish is not in Nova-3 Medical Multilingual. Reported pricing (third party): about $0.0043/min batch and $0.0077/min streaming. — [Deepgram blog](https://deepgram.com/learn/deepgram-expands-nova-3-with-german-dutch-swedish-and-danish-support); [Deepgram language detection docs](https://developers.deepgram.com/docs/language-detection); [convertaudiototext pricing](https://convertaudiototext.com/blog/deepgram-nova-3-explained)
- **AssemblyAI (vendor benchmark):** Universal-3.6 Pro Realtime lists Swedish among 32 languages. Swedish FLEURS WER is 6.6% (AssemblyAI) vs 9.9% (Speechmatics Enhanced). Swedish Common Voice is 4.8% vs 4.0%, so Speechmatics wins there. In its Pipecat test, median realtime latency is 335 ms (Universal-3.5 Pro) vs 495 ms (Speechmatics). Older docs listed Universal-Streaming Multilingual as covering only EN/ES/FR/DE/IT/PT, so the sources conflict. — [AssemblyAI benchmarks](https://www.assemblyai.com/benchmarks); [AssemblyAI Swedish page](https://www.assemblyai.com/languages/swedish); [AssemblyAI vs Speechmatics](https://www.assemblyai.com/compare/speechmatics-vs-assemblyai); [Telnyx STT models](https://developers.telnyx.com/docs/voice/stt/models)
- **Speechmatics:** supports Swedish in real time (55+ streaming languages). It launched a Swedish medical model claiming 3.91% keyword error rate vs 6.05% for AssemblyAI Universal (vendor figure). Its guidance suggests `max_delay` of about 1.5 s for voice agents (default 4 s, per a Deepgram comparison). — [Speechmatics Swedish](https://www.speechmatics.com/speech-to-text/swedish); [Speechmatics Swedish medical model](https://www.speechmatics.com/company/articles-and-news/speechmatics-launches-new-swedish-medical-model-cutting-transcription-errors); [Deepgram comparison](https://deepgram.com/learn/deepgram-vs-speechmatics-vs-assemblyai)
- **ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Realtime:** covers 90 languages including Swedish. Its overall AA-WER is 2.3%, with no Swedish breakdown. In AssemblyAI's own multilingual comparison, Scribe v2 averaged 8.77% WER vs Deepgram Nova-3 Multilingual at 12.22% (async), and 7.78% vs 8.64% (realtime voice-agent audio). Not Swedish-specific. — [FutureAGI substack](https://futureagi.substack.com/p/speech-to-text-apis-in-2026-benchmarks); [AssemblyAI accuracy blog](https://www.assemblyai.com/blog/how-accurate-speech-to-text)
- **OpenAI gpt-4o-transcribe:** supports Swedish. OpenAI claims it "matches or outperforms other leading models across most major languages" on FLEURS but publishes no reproducible per-language table. A forum user found WER 0.4–0.6 points worse than reported. Nearest Nordic datapoint: Danish FLEURS 7.84% WER on a community Danish ASR leaderboard. — [OpenAI announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-our-next-generation-audio-models/); [OpenAI community thread](https://community.openai.com/t/reproducing-gpt-4o-transcribe-fleurs-results/1151187); [Danish ASR leaderboard PR](https://github.com/Rye-A1/danish-asr-leaderboard/pull/88); [opentranscription.io](https://opentranscription.io/blog/gpt-4o-transcribe.html)
- **Google Chirp 3 (STT):** Google documents Chirp 3 transcription as "enhanced multilingual accuracy". I did not retrieve Swedish-specific STT numbers. — [Google Chirp 3 STT docs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/speech-to-text/docs/models/chirp-3)
- English-only reference (not Swedish): in the Pipecat/Daily STT benchmark, Azure scored 1.18% WER, Deepgram nova-3-general 1.62% and Google latest-long 2.85%. One review notes no independent peer-reviewed benchmark compares the big three on telephony audio. — [Coval](https://www.coval.ai/blog/best-speech-to-text-providers-in-2026-independent-benchmarks-and-how-to-choose/)
- General telephony accuracy is estimated at roughly 80–88% across STT systems (low-quality, non-language-specific estimate). — [FutureAGI](https://futureagi.substack.com/p/speech-to-text-apis-in-2026-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- For a Halmstad firm, Azure sv-SE remains a defensible default: EU data residency (Sweden Central), recently updated sv-SE model, and post-stream refinement now available for Swedish. The highest-value experiment is an A/B test on the firm's own 8 kHz call recordings: Azure sv-SE (with phrase lists for client names and terms like "moms", "deklaration", "årsredovisning", "K10", "bokslut") vs Deepgram Nova-3 sv vs ElevenLabs Scribe v2 RT vs AssemblyAI Universal-3.6.
- KB-Whisper is the best open Swedish ASR on wideband benchmarks. It could be self-hosted in the EU for post-call transcripts and summaries, and maybe for streaming via chunked or faster-whisper setups at the cost of latency. It was not evaluated on 8 kHz telephony, and its training data is mostly broadcast/parliament/subtitle audio.
- Swedish/English code-switching (callers with English names, firm names) favours engines with explicit bilingual modes. Azure's August 2026 update mentions keeping English available for bilingual use.

### Gaps
- No Swedish telephony (8 kHz) WER benchmark found for any engine.
- No retrieved Swedish WER for Google Chirp 3, Azure sv-SE or gpt-4o-transcribe.
- No independent latency measurements for Swedish streaming.

## 4. Swedish text-to-speech (TTS): quality, latency, pricing

### Takeaway
Swedish TTS options in 2026: Azure neural sv-SE (Sofie, Mattias, Hillevi; standard neural, with no confirmed sv-SE HD/DragonHD or Omni voice), Google Chirp 3 HD sv-SE (GA since July 2025, streaming, supports mu-law/A-law output), Cartesia Sonic 3.x (Swedish supported, under 90 ms first audio claimed) and ElevenLabs (Swedish marketed with regional accents). I found no independent Swedish naturalness (MOS) comparison. Google Chirp 3 HD and ElevenLabs/Cartesia are the most likely upgrades over Azure's older sv-SE neural voices, but this needs a blind listening test with native speakers.

### Cited Findings
- Azure sv-SE voices: SofieNeural (female), MattiasNeural (male), HilleviNeural (female). Microsoft's February 2025 update lists Mattias and Sofie under "GA: Other quality improvements", not as HD. — [Azure TTS Feb 2025 update](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/azure-ai-foundry-blog/azure-ai-speech-text-to-speech-feb-2025-updates-new-hd-voices-and-more/4387263); [Azure language support](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/speech-service/language-support); [Azure neural voices expansion (Hillevi)](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/azure-ai-foundry-blog/azure-neural-text-to-speech-updates-51-new-voices-added-to-the-portfolio/1988418)
- Azure HD/DragonHD: the language-support page lists sv-SE only under Personal Voice and Visemes, not HD. Dragon HD Omni (preview, 700+ voices) is documented as multilingual with auto-detection and SSML `<lang>`, but released Omni voices are en-US and zh-CN. A Microsoft Q&A user reports Omni is "currently available only for a limited set of locales, primarily en-US and selected zh-CN", with no ETA for others (unofficial). ## 5. Swedish LLM quality for customer conversation

### Takeaway
I could not retrieve a current EuroEval (formerly ScandEval) Swedish leaderboard snapshot. ### Cited Findings

- A EuroEval table in the Bielik 11B v2 report (2025) includes a Swedish column covering Gemini, GPT-4o and Gemma. Gemini-1.5-Pro-002 scores 1.29 for Swedish. — [arXiv 2505.02410](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.02410)

### Inferences
- For a receptionist, the main LLM requirements are short, idiomatic Swedish replies, reliable tool calling, and low time-to-first-token. Benchmark rank matters less than these. An A/B test against a small Claude or Gemini model on transcribed real calls, scored by native speakers, would be more informative than leaderboards.
- Nordic open models (GPT-SW3, Viking) are older generations and lack the tool-calling maturity voice agents need. They matter mainly if self-hosting in the EU is required.

## 6. Swedish regulatory and consumer context

### Takeaway
(a) Recording or transcribing a call you take part in is not a crime in Sweden. GDPR still requires informing the caller, a legal basis, purpose limitation, retention limits and a DPA with processors. IMY's 2026 sandbox report on AI transcription (social services) accepts AI transcription with human oversight and data minimisation. (b) The EU AI Act's Article 50 chatbot disclosure duty applied from **2 August 2026** and was not delayed by the Digital Omnibus, so the AI receptionist must say it is an AI. (c) For auditors, **26 § revisorslagen (2001:883)** on confidentiality creates real legal uncertainty about sending client data to external AI or cloud vendors. FAR asked the government in April 2026 to review it. (d) Swedish callers still strongly prefer phone with a human for complex or financial matters.

### Cited Findings
- **Recording legality:** recording a call you take part in is legal without consent. Brottsbalken 4:9 a covers only calls you are not part of. GDPR requires information, legal basis (consent or legitimate interest), purpose limitation, storage minimisation and security, plus a personuppgiftsbiträdesavtal (DPA) with AI vendors. The typical practice is an automatic message at call start. These sources are vendors, not IMY. — [Sono](https://callsono.com/sv/blog/far-man-spela-in-kundsamtal/); [Telink](https://telink.se/funktioner/vad-sager-lagen-om-samtalsinspelning/); [Infinity/Infracom docs](https://docs.icc.infracom.se/docs/sv/vilka-regler-g%C3%A4ller-f%C3%B6r-inspelning-av-samtal)
- **IMY (Swedish DPA):** its innovation-sandbox report with Kalmar kommun (published around April 2026) found a GDPR legal basis for AI transcription in social services. Conditions: human control, robust security, audio and transcript kept only as long as needed for review (accuracy vs storage-minimisation tension), and review close in time to the call. Summaries that only compress the conversation without conclusions are **not automated decision-making**. — [IMY report PDF](https://www.imy.se/globalassets/dokument/rapporter/transkribering-inom-socialtjansten.pdf); [CIO Analytics, April 2026](https://www.cioanalytics.se/artikel/2026/04/imy-oppnar-upp-for-ai-transkribering-i-socialtjansten/); [techlaw.se](https://techlaw.se/sverige-imy-bedomer-rattsliga-forutsattningar-for-ai-transkribering-i-socialtjansten/); [Delphi](https://www.delphi.se/sv/tech-blog/ai-transkribering-i-socialtjansten-ny-rapport-fran-imys-innovationssandlada/)
- **EU AI Act Article 50:** transparency duties, including informing people they are interacting with an AI, apply from **2 August 2026**. The Digital Omnibus (described as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force 27 July 2026) postponed the high-risk deadlines (Annex III to 2 December 2027, Annex I to 2 August 2028). It gave only a narrow grace period to **2 December 2026** for Art. 50(2) machine-readable marking of systems already on the market. Chatbot disclosure was not delayed. Sources predating formal adoption describe the Omnibus as provisional, so check EUR-Lex. — [Jones Walker](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai-law-blog/yes-august-2-still-matters-the-eu-approved-a-high-risk-ai-delay-but-most-trans.html); [Usercentrics](https://usercentrics.com/knowledge-hub/eu-ai-act-high-risk-delay-article-50-transparency-consent/); [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/); [CSA research note](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-article50-watermarking-deadline/); [Cobalt](https://www.cobalt.legal/news-cases/digital-omnibus-on-ai/)
- **Auditor confidentiality (revisorslagen 26 §):** FAR (April 2026) says 26 § creates "great uncertainty" about whether, and how, client data may be used in digital/AI tools. The preparatory works give little guidance. Internal use under the firm's control fits more easily. Use of external vendors, which small and mid-size firms depend on, raises the risk of "obehörigt röjande" (unauthorised disclosure). FAR has asked the government to review the provision. Balans says firms need processes and controls protecting confidential data from unauthorised access. A thesis notes bokföringslagen and revisorslagen complicate cloud or foreign-server storage. — [FAR, April 2026](https://www.far.se/aktuellt/nyheter/2026/april/revisorslagen-hammar-utvecklingen-av-digitala-verktyg/); [Balans opinion](https://tidningenbalans.se/opinion/tystnadsplikten-far-inte-bli-ett-hinder-for-framtidens-revision/); [Balans: AI och regelefterlevnad](https://tidningenbalans.se/fordjupning/ai-och-regelefterlevnad-vad-bor-byran-tanka-pa/); [Balans: Digitaliseringsgruppen](https://tidningenbalans.se/nyheter/framtidens-revision-digitaliseringsgruppen-vagleder-om-ai/); [SLU thesis](https://stud.epsilon.slu.se/21358/1/sigfridsson-e-vistr%C3%B6m-a-20250701.pdf); [Revisorsinspektionen FAQ](https://www.revisorsinspektionen.se/publikationer/vanliga-fragor-och-svar/ansvar-och-uppgifter/)
- **Swedish caller expectations:**
  - Almost half of Swedes (46%, Svenska folket och AI, Gothenburg University, 2022) prefer AI customer service in some situations, with younger people more positive. — [GU](https://www.gu.se/nyheter/svenska-folket-och-ai-vad-vill-vi-vad-tanker-vi); [SVT](https://www.svt.se/nyheter/vetenskap/ny-rapport-manga-foredrar-ai-kundtjanst-framfor-mansklig)
  - EY 2026: 53% of Swedes prefer phone support (global average 47%), and 49% find it hard to explain a problem without talking to a human. — [EY 2026](https://www.ey.com/sv_se/newsroom/2026/01/fyra-av-tio-unga-foredrar-ai-nar-de-soker-och-jamfor)
  - Visma Amili 2025: only 6% of 18–34-year-olds prefer AI for invoice and payment questions. — [Dagens Teknik](https://www.dagensteknik.se/svenskarna-frustrerade-pa-kundtjanst-men-ai-chatbotar-far-inget-fortroende/); [CIO Analytics, June 2025](https://www.cioanalytics.se/artikel/2025/06/svenskarna-skeptiska-ai-i-kundtjanst-en-riskfaktor-for-finansbolag/)
  - ServiceNow: 64% of Swedes say AI chatbots have little or no ability to perceive emotions. — [Dagens Teknik](https://www.dagensteknik.se/svenskarna-frustrerade-pa-kundtjanst-men-ai-chatbotar-far-inget-fortroende/); [Computer Sweden](https://computersweden.se/article/2086449/ai-i-all-ara-manga-svenska-kunder-vill-fortfarande-prata-med-en-manniska.html)

### Inferences
- Every call should start with a short Swedish disclosure, for example "Du pratar med [firmans] AI-assistent; samtalet transkriberas…". This covers both Art. 50 and GDPR information duties. Offer a quick path to a human ("säg 'människa' eller tryck 0").
- For tystnadsplikt, keep the receptionist's role to message-taking, routing and booking, and avoid discussing client engagement details unless the caller is verified. Keep all processing within EU-resident, DPA-covered services (Azure Sweden Central / EU Data Zone already helps). Document the 26 § assessment. The AI should not read out case status, which is the feature Svaria markets, without strong caller verification and a documented legal assessment.
- Low acceptance of AI for money and invoice questions among Swedes suggests the receptionist should hand off quickly when callers raise substantive tax or audit matters.

### Gaps
- No IMY guidance specific to businesses' AI phone agents or call recording was found; only the social-services sandbox report.
- Sweden's national market-surveillance authority for the AI Act and the exact Art. 50(1) wording ("obvious from context" exception) were not retrieved.
- PTS rules on caller-ID spoofing and number presentation were not retrieved.

## 7. Telephony in Sweden: Twilio, 46elks, Telnyx, Sinch

### Takeaway
Twilio supports inbound and outbound voice on Swedish numbers, but local numbers need a regulatory bundle with a business address in the number's area code region plus proof such as a registreringsbevis. Outbound caller ID is "not guaranteed". Telnyx requires Requirement Groups (since 16 September 2024), proof of a Swedish address and physical presence. 46elks (Swedish) is cheapest for SIP: 0 öre/min to SIP/WebRTC and 10 öre/min to an external SIP trunk. Swedish SIP-trunk providers (Cellip, Gotanet) are alternatives for a PBX-style trunk.

### Cited Findings
- **Twilio Sweden:** inbound and outbound domestic and international calling supported. Outbound caller ID "local (not guaranteed)". Local business numbers require an address in the locality matching the number's prefix (no PO Box), with proof such as a registreringsbevis, utility bill, tax notice or rent receipt. Error 21615 occurs if no qualifying address is on file. Prefixes listed: mobile +467, toll-free +46200, national +4610. — [Twilio SE regulatory](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/guidelines/se/regulatory); [Twilio SE voice](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/guidelines/se/voice); [Twilio error 21615](https://www.twilio.com/docs/api/errors/21615); [Twilio address requirements](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/223182868-Understanding-Address-Requirements-for-Phone-Numbers)
- Twilio bundle review takes "up to two business days" per one help article, or "several weeks" in some regions per the console guide. — [Twilio bundle help](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/8338625205147-How-to-Submit-a-Regulatory-Bundle-for-Phone-Number-Regulatory-Compliance); [Twilio console guide](https://www.twilio.com/docs/phone-numbers/regulatory/getting-started/console-create-new-bundle)
- Twilio porting in Sweden: geographic, toll-free and national numbers can be ported. It needs an LOA dated within 90 days plus a recent bill, and takes about 4–6 weeks. — [Twilio SE porting](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/guidelines/se/porting)
- Twilio Elastic SIP Trunking (Sweden page): termination from **$0.0147/min**. A search extract claims the page says "voice-enabled numbers aren't available in this locale" for SIP trunking. This conflicts with Programmable Voice numbers being available, so verify it. — [Twilio SIP trunking pricing SE](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/sip-trunking/pricing/se)
- **46elks (Stockholm):** calls routed to SIP/WebRTC cost **0 öre/min**, to an external SIP trunk **10 öre/min**, and calls to Swedish numbers from about 15 öre/min (an unclear 57 öre figure also appears). A SIP number costs 30 kr/month (older tutorial). — [46elks pricing Sweden](https://46elks.se/pricing/sweden); [46elks SIP tutorial](https://46elks.se/tutorials/ta-emot-telefonsamtal-med-sip)
- **Telnyx:** Requirement Groups mandatory for Swedish numbers since 16 September 2024. Businesses need an incorporation certificate, authorised-representative details and a Swedish address with proof dated within 3 months. End users must be physically present in Sweden. Local numbers from about $1/month. Generic SIP rates are about $0.005/min outbound and $0.0032/min inbound (likely US rates; Swedish rates not retrieved). The Voice API adds a $0.002/min platform fee. — [Telnyx Sweden DID requirements](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/1311477-sweden-did-requirements); [Telnyx Sweden numbers](https://telnyx.com/phone-numbers/sweden); [Telnyx pricing](https://telnyx.com/pricing); [Telnyx elastic SIP pricing](https://telnyx.com/pricing/elastic-sip)
- **Swedish SIP-trunk providers:** Cellip SIP-Trunk Bas costs 296 kr/month plus 19.50 kr per number (excl. VAT). Gotanet costs 100 kr/month (4 channels), 200 kr (10) or 500 kr (30), plus a one-time 500 kr. — [Cellip](https://www.cellip.com/tjanster/sip-trunk-bas/); [Gotanet](https://www.gotanet.se/foretag/telefoni/sip-trunk/)
- **Sinch:** Voice Relay (early access, March 2026) for AI agents on phone calls. I found no Swedish SIP or number pricing. — [Sinch news](https://sinch.com/news/sinch-launches-voice-relay-to-bring-ai-agents-a-voice/)

### Inferences
- For a Halmstad firm (area code 035), a Twilio local 035 number needs a Halmstad business address. The simpler path is to keep the firm's existing number on its PBX and forward to the AI DID (section 2).
- 46elks is the cheapest Sweden-native, EU-based option for routing a Swedish number into LiveKit SIP: 0 öre/min to SIP, with a Swedish company and invoicing in SEK. It is a credible Twilio replacement if data residency or cost matters. Verify 46elks' support for SIP headers and caller-ID passthrough into LiveKit.

### Gaps
- Twilio inbound per-minute prices for Swedish local numbers and monthly number fees were not retrieved.
- Sinch Swedish voice/SIP pricing was not retrieved.
- Whether 46elks and Telnyx pass the original caller ID on calls forwarded from a PBX was not confirmed.
