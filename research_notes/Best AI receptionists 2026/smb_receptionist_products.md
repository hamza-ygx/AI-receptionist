## Q1. Product landscape and which products target accounting/legal firms

### Takeaway
The market splits into (a) standalone SMB AI receptionists priced per call/caller/minute (Smith.ai AI, Goodcall, Rosie, Upfirst, Sameday, Slang, Numa), (b) AI agents bolted onto business phone/UCaaS/helpdesk platforms (Aircall, Dialpad, RingCentral AIR, Nextiva XBert, Quo Sona, Zendesk), and (c) general agent builders (Lindy, Jotform). Smith.ai is by far the most legal/accounting-oriented; Ruby is still a human service with AI assist, not an AI receptionist.

## Q2. Core capabilities (FAQ/KB, booking, transfers, messages, intake, spam, after-hours, voicemail)

### Takeaway
Table stakes in 2026: auto-training from website (and Google Business Profile), calendar booking (Google/Outlook/Calendly), warm transfer with context, call summaries pushed by email/SMS (and Slack/Teams at Smith.ai), spam blocking, and recording+transcription of every call. Differentiators are human escalation, CRM write-back depth, waterfall/sequential transfers, and SMS follow-ups/booking links sent during the call.

### Gaps

- Voicemail-handling specifics (AI voicemail transcription vs AI replacing voicemail) only confirmed for Ruby (voicemail transcripts).

## Q6. Human fallback and escalation logic

### Takeaway
Smith.ai's hybrid (AI first, live US-based receptionists as escalation) is the distinctive model; all platforms offer transfer-to-human with context. Billing for escalation is a source of complaints.

### Cited Findings
- Smith.ai: AI-initiated escalation to live receptionist stated as free on pricing/help content; homepage lists a $3 human escalation charge — sources conflict — [Smith.ai pricing](https://smith.ai/pricing/ai-receptionist); [smith.ai](https://smith.ai/)
- Smith.ai human-staffed plans: from $300/mo for 30 calls (third-party Sept 2026) — [schedulingkit Smith.ai pricing](https://schedulingkit.com/pricing-guides/smith-ai-pricing)
- Smith.ai Trustpilot complaint: AI transferred calls to live agents without consent, raising bill; cancellation difficult — [Trustpilot Smith.ai](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/smith.ai)
- Rosie: warm/live transfers and waterfall transfers (Growth) — [heyrosie.com/pricing](https://heyrosie.com/pricing)
- Goodcall: transfer with backup if no answer; else message/callback/self-service link — [goodcall.com](https://www.goodcall.com/)
- Lindy: forwarded calls stop LLM charges once handed to a human — [Lindy phone pricing](https://www.lindy.ai/academy-lessons/lindy-phone-pricing)
- Dialpad/Aircall/Zendesk: escalate with full context — [Dialpad AI receptionist](https://www.dialpad.com/solutions/ai-receptionist/); [Aircall help](https://support.aircall.io/en-gb/articles/25979264664221); [Zendesk GA](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/11183058023450-Announcing-the-general-availability-of-voice-AI-agents)
